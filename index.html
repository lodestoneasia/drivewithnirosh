Questions

Straight questions. Straight answers.

Prefer to call?

Find me at Volvo Glenmarie, Shah Alam, Selangor. Message ahead and I'll make sure I'm there.

Where are you based, and do you serve Subang Jaya?

I'm at Volvo Glenmarie in Glenmarie, Shah Alam, a short drive from Subang Jaya, USJ, Petaling Jaya and Puchong. Test drives can also start from your home or office where I can arrange it.

How much is the booking fee, and who do I pay?

It depends on the model and is confirmed in writing before you pay anything. You always pay Volvo Glenmarie directly and get an official receipt, never a personal account.

What's the free surprise reveal?

Every Volvo I deliver comes with The Reveal: the car under a cover, a ribbon bow, a personal card and photos on the day, on me. If you're buying for someone special, there are bigger celebration packages from RM1,300.

Do I deal with you or with the dealership?

With me, from the first message to the day you drive off. Volvo Glenmarie handles the paperwork, loan submission and servicing, and I stay your contact throughout.

Is a test drive at my home or office possible?

Often, yes. Tell me your area and the cars you want to try, and I'll confirm what I can arrange. If it has to be the showroom, I'll plan a route that matches your daily drive.

Will you keep messaging me if I don't buy?

No. If you go quiet I'll follow up once, and that's it. You're welcome back whenever you're ready.

Can I use my own bank and insurer?

Yes. I'll show you the options I have so you can compare, but the choice is yours.

Does an EV make sense if I live in a condo?

Often, yes. It depends on whether your JMB allows a wall charger and whether you can charge at work or a mall you visit regularly. Tell me your building and routine and I'll give you an honest answer, including when a hybrid is the better choice.

How long will I wait for my car?

It depends on the model, variant and colour. I'll check live stock and give you a realistic date before you pay a booking fee.

Can you help with my trade-in?

Yes. I'll get you a valuation and explain how it's worked out, so you can compare it with other offers.
